Two ways in

The first run is small and goes out direct. If you want a can, tell us where to send it. If you build, supply, fund or study things like this, tell us which and we will send the package that fits.

For drinkers

No spam. We will write when cans exist, and not before. Submission is delivered by Formspree; do not include confidential, financial, medical, or authentication information.

For partners

Submission is delivered by Formspree; do not include confidential, financial, medical, or authentication information. Formulation detail is released only after a mutual NDA; architecture, evidence, and grant materials can be shared without one.
