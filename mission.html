The constitutional rule

The Mandate of Mistrust

No claim, source, model, operator, or automation receives authority merely by assertion. Authority requires evidence, provenance, and human authorization. Black American distrust of medical research is an evidence-based response to documented history; participation must therefore be earned through consent, transparency, and shared benefit rather than requested through marketing alone.

Tuskegee, 1932–1972

For forty years the US Public Health Service observed hundreds of Black men with syphilis while withholding effective treatment, even after penicillin became standard. It is the direct reason informed consent and IRB oversight were strengthened nationally.

Henrietta Lacks, 1951

Cells taken from a Black woman without her knowledge became one of the most commercially valuable cell lines in history. Her family went uninformed and uncompensated for decades. The lesson is data and biosample sovereignty.

Exclusion, ongoing

Black Americans remain significantly underrepresented in clinical trials, including nutrition and supplement research. The evidence base products rely on does not represent this population — and correcting that is itself a scientific contribution.

The contribution

Structural, not biological

This is the distinction the whole mission rests on, and we state it plainly. The health disparities affecting Black Americans are real, large and well documented — and they are driven by structural racism, unequal healthcare access, environmental exposure and chronic-stress load. Not by genetic difference. That framing is both the scientific consensus and the foundation of our credibility: conditions are addressable, which is precisely why research and access can help.
