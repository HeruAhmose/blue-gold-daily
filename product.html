Discipline

What is deliberately not in it

No CBD or THC

Hemp inputs are restricted to hulled seed, seed protein and seed oil. Novel hemp materials stay in the research lane until they have their own clearance.

No gardenia blue

It is made by reacting genipin with soy protein hydrolysate, which would add an allergen, and it is less stable through heat and acid than Galdieria.

No cacao

Beautiful, and wrong for a clear RTD tea. It is reserved for a Blue-Gold Cacao Elixir where it can be the base rather than a complication.

No disease claims

Structure/function language only, on the label and everywhere else. The trial tells us what it tells us and nothing more.
