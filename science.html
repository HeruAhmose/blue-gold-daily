The line

Proven, and not yet

Our own technical package states this on its face, so that no partner mistakes a working figure for a researched one.

Verified

Holds up to a source

  • Every regulatory citation, checked against eCFR and the Federal Register
  • The saffron dose and its standardisation, including the trademark and patent position
  • The comparative stability of Galdieria against spirulina phycocyanin
  • The aging-biology framework, the microbiome trial we cite, and the heterologous crocetin titres

Working target

Not yet a finding

  • Every ingredient quantity except the saffron dose — these are bench targets a formulator will overwrite
  • Colourant loading ranges, which depend entirely on a supplier's measured colour strength
  • All cost and budget figures, which are planning estimates and not quotes
  • Branded ingredient specifications carried from general knowledge, to be confirmed supplier by supplier
